很迟钝地发现，其实我所记录的东西大多是 Myself Only 的，可能浏览这个博客的人并不能从其中收获太多，至少我自己觉得，绝大多数时候我的想法是很奇怪的，再加上我喜欢用很多隐晦的话，我总是能感觉到，行人们品尝不到什么友好的味道。而我渐渐明白，其实之所以想法不同，或是不能理解、接受某一种想法，原因大抵是出于不同的信仰。

“信仰”这个词往往是很模糊的。比如“我信仰马克思主义”，这样的话至少在我身边的人口中不过是一种敷衍，或是一种道理上的信任。我曾见到过非常虔诚的佛教徒，在北方寒冷的冬天身披百衲衣徒步行走在路上——在我看来这信仰倒是极为真实的，至少它足够成为一个人生命的理由。

如果我可以为一样东西献出生命，那么我就可以说，这是我的信仰。“生命诚可贵，爱情价更高。若为自由故，二者皆可抛。”中文是如此简单以至于说这句话的人似乎忘了这第一句所占的分量——生命是如此可贵，除了信仰，我想没有什么可以成为我托付生命的理由。

我曾经信仰爱情，并体会着这种信仰给我带来的希望与幸福感；后来我信仰初心，墨守成规地在不断的失去中告慰自己。直到现在，我意识到，信仰往往是有害的。人不能没有信仰，但信仰应当是*隐存*的——用心灵哲学的一个术语来说。明确的信仰往往会让人奋不顾身，成为忘记“生命诚可贵”的狂热分子。

> 我已经失败了，一切都失败了。在不远的将来我们可能有必要亲手结束自己的生命。——吉姆·琼斯

人的一生往往是一次奇遇，会在某个时间信仰某项事物，又在某一刻开始怀疑，然后抛弃。最高的信仰应该是不明确的，把生命建立在一个确定的事物上，希望就会随着时过境迁分崩离析。没有退路的生命是可怕的。

It delayed too much for me to realize that, things I recorded actually *Myself Only* mostly. People who looked through this blog did not get too much, which I think at least. Most of my ideas are strange, as well as I used a lot of obscure. Always, I feel that passers-by could not enjoy friendly taste much. And I gradually realize that, in fact, what causes a different idea , or misunderstanding or non-acception, is probably different belief.

"Belief" is a word which is too blurred mostly. For example, " I believe in Marxism", at least in the population of my side is nothing but a perfunctory, at most a faith. I have seen a very devout Buddhist, walking on the road with a thin coat in the cold northern winter, which in my opinion is actually real belief, at least it will be a reason for someone's life .

I can say that "This is my belief" if I **could have given up** my life for something. "Life is dear, love is dearer. Both can be given up for freedom." Chinese is so simple that people who say the proverbs seem to forget how important the first part is. Life is so valuable that I do not think anything could be the reason I entrust life in addition to the belief.

I have believed in love, and experience with such hope and happiness the belief brought me; later I believed in the *initial resolution*, and comfort myself invariably with lost in the constant. Now I realized that, belief is often harmful. People can not live without belief, but belief should be *inexistant* - a term used for the philosophy of mind. Explicit beliefs tend to make people desperate to become fanatics who had forgot "life is  dear".

> I have failed. All is lost. The time is not far off when it will become necessary for us to die by our own hands. - Jim Jones

Human's life is often an adventure, an experience of believing at a time, and beginning to doubt and abandon in a moment later. Highest belief should not be clear. While the life is based on a certain thing, hope will fall apart with the passage of time. Life is terrible without leeway.
